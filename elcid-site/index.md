# EL CID Country Club

Benidoleig · Marina Alta · Costa Blanca, Spain

EL CID Country Club is a small rural hotel with a restaurant, outdoor pool, terraces and guest accommodation in Benidoleig, Alicante.

## Stay

- Double room with terrace.
- Family room.
- Independent studio with kitchen.
- Outdoor pool, terraces, tennis, hiking and cycling access are presented on the public site.

Live availability, prices, cancellation conditions and included services must be confirmed through the booking option displayed on the website for the selected dates.

The public website presents continental breakfast, daytime service, dinners and private tables. Service times are confirmed with the reservation.
