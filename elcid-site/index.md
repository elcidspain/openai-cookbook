# EL CID Country Club

Benidoleig · Marina Alta · Costa Blanca, Spain

EL CID Country Club is a country-club hospitality property in Benidoleig, Alicante, combining guest accommodation, an independent studio, restaurant service and outdoor spaces between the Marina Alta mountains and the Mediterranean coast.

- Double room with terrace.
- Family room.
- Independent studio with kitchen.
- Outdoor pool and terraces.
- Tennis.
- Access to hiking and cycling routes across Marina Alta.
